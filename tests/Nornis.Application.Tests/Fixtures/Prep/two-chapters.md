# Scene 1: The Customs House

### Goal

The party learns that the Silver Key was pulled from the harbor mud this morning.

### Read-aloud

> Fog sits on the water like wet wool. Somewhere a bell rings twice and stops.
>
> A clerk with ink to the elbows looks up and does not smile.

### Likely questions

> [!ASK]
> Who on your crew has been to Black Harbor before, and what do they owe here?

### End state

The clerk names Captain Voss as the man who brought the key ashore.

# Scene 2: The Mudlark

### Goal

Meet Captain Voss and decide whether to trust him.

### Read-aloud

> The Mudlark lists to port. Her captain is mending a sail with a fishhook.

> [!PROP]
> A tide table, folded small. Tomorrow's low water is circled in red.
> Underneath, in pencil: *bring the key*.

### Likely questions

Why did Voss keep the key a secret? He didn't; nobody asked.

### End state

Voss agrees to row them out at low water.
