# Session 5 — The beacon and the barge

Hard travel: down from Greyhollow Abbey, south across the downs past the
Cinder Tor, making for Saltmere ahead of the dredge barge. We did not make it
ahead of anything.

- From the downs at dusk the party saw it: a beacon burning on the Cinder Tor,
  where nothing has been built in living memory. Green-hearted flame. Elowen
  says it is a calling-fire — "someone telling the water where to meet them."
- Split the difference badly: went for the beacon first. The tor's crown is a
  ring of old fire-scarred stones and the fresh beacon was tended by
  Groundlings — Pike Salloway himself among them, which means the Crew's
  chief thought this worth his own neck.
- Fight on the tor in the dark. Bram dropped the beacon frame with two axe
  strokes; Salloway broke his knee coming off the rocks and his crew left
  him. We have him.
- Salloway's confession, once Corvin explained his arithmetic of charges:
  the Crew was hired through the Salt Factor, but the pay is keep-minted
  silver — fresh-struck, Bleakspire's own stamp. "Wreckers' wages from the
  Castellan's own mint, and you lot still think the danger's an old monk."
  He says the light and lens from Lanternwatch went north on a barge flying
  keep colors. He never saw who commanded it and is glad of it.
- Saltmere, too late: the fishing town watched the dredge barge come in at
  slack tide, met by armed men "in good order — soldiers, not wreckers," and
  something the size of a hay wagon swung ashore under canvas. Cart train
  gone north by the coast road before midnight. Toward the mountains. Toward
  the Keep.
- The party is now openly saying what the notes have danced around: Castellan
  Maren Voss's name is on the silver, the barge, and the road north. Saying
  it and proving it are different things, and if we are wrong we are accusing
  the one power in the Reach that could actually help us.

Cliffhanger: the Vesper Bell is raised and ashore, moving under guard toward
Bleakspire Keep, and the spring tide — Harrach's letters call it the
Undertide — is eleven days out. Salloway goes to Crane in Harrowport under
guard. Next session: the Keep.
