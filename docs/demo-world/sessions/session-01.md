# Session 1 — The dark lantern

Third wreck in a month on the Vespergale coast, and Harbormaster Ilsabet Crane
is done waiting for the Castellan's riders. She hired the party in the back
room of the customs house — Corvin Hale's old office, which he did not enjoy —
to find out why the Lanternwatch light has been dark for nine nights and where
its keeper has gone.

Party: Kestrel Vane, Bram Ottery, Elowen Marsh, Corvin Hale.

- Walked the shore road west out of Harrowport to the Lanternwatch headland.
  Kestrel found cart tracks leaving the lighthouse path, heavy-laden, three
  days old at most.
- The lighthouse itself: door forced, lamp-room stripped. The great lens is
  GONE — not smashed, removed. Whoever took it knew the fittings. Old
  Fennick's supper was still on the table. No blood, no body.
- Bram identified fresh caulk marks on the landing stage below the headland: a
  barge moored here recently, a big one.
- On the climb down, Elowen found a wrecker's mark scratched into the rock —
  the kind the Groundling Crew used to leave back when Pike Salloway ran
  wrecks off Saltmere. Thought they'd disbanded years ago. Apparently not.
- Back in Harrowport by dark. Crane confirmed the wrecks started the same week
  the light went out; all three ships broke on the same reef line the lantern
  used to cover.
- One more thread: a hooded broker the dockhands call the Salt Factor has been
  buying drowned charts — old survey maps of the river mouth and the flooded
  crossing upriver — and paying in good silver. Nobody has seen his face.
  Corvin's old contacts say he's been at it since early Rainmonth.

Crane is paying the party to keep pulling. Next step: the Salt Factor's silver
trail leads upriver toward Windrow. Fennick is officially missing, not dead —
Elowen was firm about that and nobody argued with her.
