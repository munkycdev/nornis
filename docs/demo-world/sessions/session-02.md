# Session 2 — Lights under the water

Upriver after the Salt Factor's silver. The road follows the Silverwithe out
of Harrowport through the water-meadows to Windrow.

- Windrow: farm village, one mill, suspicious of strangers until Bram fixed
  the mill sluice in an afternoon. After that everyone talked at once.
- The miller's son, Tam Rush, has seen lights under the water at the old
  flooded crossing north of the village — the one the maps call the Sunken
  Ferry — three nights running. Green, moving, "like lanterns walking on the
  bottom." His father told him to stop rowing up there. He did not stop.
- Tam guided the party upriver at dusk. He's maybe fourteen and braver than
  sense allows; we are keeping him.
- The Sunken Ferry: drowned pilings, a sunken causeway, and a working dredge
  crew where no dredge crew should be. Flat-bottomed barge, grapples, a
  diving bell of all things. Kestrel counted nine men. Groundling Crew marks
  on the barge, same as the Lanternwatch scratch.
- It went to blades when Corvin got recognized — one of the dredgers is a man
  he once fined for smuggling. Short ugly fight in the shallows. The crew cut
  anchor and poled off downriver rather than finish it, which tells you they
  are paid to dig, not to fight.
- What they left behind: a salvaged bronze fitting, old, big as a shield boss,
  stamped with a bell within a ring of waves. Elowen would not touch it with
  bare hands. She says it hums when the water moves.
- Also recovered: a waxed satchel with a drowned chart of the crossing —
  one of the Salt Factor's purchases, marked with dig lines. Whatever they
  are dredging for, they have nearly found it.

Camped above the crossing. Elowen sat up with the bronze fitting wrapped in
her coat and did not sleep well. Next: Thornhollow, where Tam says the Widow
Harrach knows every old mark on the river — including, maybe, the bell.
