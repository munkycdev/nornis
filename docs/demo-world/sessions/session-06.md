# Session 6 — The Undertide

Bleakspire Keep, by the old drove road up from the coast. Two days' climb with
the spring tide — the Undertide — three days out by Widow Harrach's reckoning.

- Corvin's forged customs writ got the party through the outer gate as a
  salvage assessment. It survived exactly as far as the inner yard, which was
  farther than any of us bet.
- The keep's garrison is smaller than its walls pretend. Half the soldiers we
  saw at Saltmere are here; the rest of the garrison does not seem to know
  what their own cellars are holding.
- Kestrel and Elowen found the cistern stair while Bram and Corvin were being
  loudly official. Under Bleakspire Keep is a drowned undercroft — a
  cistern-crypt, older than the keep above it, cut for exactly one purpose.
  The hanging mount from the abbey foundry is bolted to its vault, and the
  Vesper Bell hangs from it, still weeping river water.
- Old Fennick is alive. Locked in the cistern cells with the Lanternwatch
  lens beside him like a trophy — Castellan Voss wanted the coast dark and
  the keeper somewhere he could not relight it, but would not kill him.
  Fennick's first words were a complaint about the food, so he is himself.
- Voss came down to the crypt with Brother Alder and found the party waiting.
  No theatrics from her, which was somehow worse. Her case, plainly made:
  she is the last heir of the Bellwardens, the wrecks were Alder's doing
  against her orders, and when the Undertide comes she means to sound the
  Bell once — "a voice, not a weapon" — and let the old coast faith speak
  again. She showed us the Bellwarden brand on her forearm like a deed of
  title.
- Elowen answered her with the chapter book: Verene's own hand, "it must not
  be heard." What broke it was Fennick, hauled blinking into the lamplight,
  telling Voss what the green calling-fire actually raised off Saltmere —
  he watched from his cell window as things came up the tide-line that the
  soldiers burned rather than name. Alder went grey. He had not known either.
- No pitched battle in the end. Alder, of all people, unhung the Bell — a
  Bellwarden's blood being needed to lift it from its mount — and it went
  into the mountain deep below the cistern, where no tide reaches and no
  barge will ever grapple it. Voss did not stop him. She watched it go down
  and said nothing at all.
- Terms, witnessed by Fennick and sealed by Crane's authority once we got
  word to Harrowport: Voss abdicates the keep to a warden council, funds the
  relighting of Lanternwatch, and answers for the wrecks before the
  Harrowport assizes. Alder surrendered. Pike Salloway will enjoy the
  company.

Epilogue, quickly: the Lanternwatch light was lit again on the night of the
Undertide, Old Fennick at the lamp with the recovered lens. Tam Rush has
informed his father he is going to be a river pilot. Elowen slept the night
through for the first time since Windrow — no bell, she says, just the river.
The Reach is quiet. The party drinks free in two towns and is banned from one
keep.
