# Session 4 — The empty grave

Greyhollow Abbey, in the foothills below the Bleakspine. Roofless nave, fallen
bell-tower — of course it had a bell-tower — and a scriptorium that survived
because its door was iron and its builders were serious people.

- The scriptorium records, where damp has spared them, tell the story Harrach
  half-knew. Forty years ago Prioress Verene ordered "the reliquary of the
  old coast faith" taken down and drowned at the crossing. Her own words, in
  the chapter book: "It cannot be broken and it must not be heard. Let the
  river hold what we could not."
- The Bellwardens — the relic's hereditary keepers — refused the drowning and
  were cast out of the order. The chapter book lists them by name. The page
  after that has been cut out with a knife. Recently: the cut is clean and
  the dust says weeks, not decades.
- The abbey's dead lie in a terraced yard behind the chapter house. Corvin,
  reading grave rows against the necrology like the customs man he is, found
  the discrepancy: Brother Alder, drowned in the flood of the abbey's last
  winter, has a marker and a necrology entry — and an empty grave. Opened
  from outside, long ago. No coffin. No Alder.
- Then the man himself, in the ruined nave, done with hiding: the Salt Factor
  put back his hood and he is Brother Alder — old, salt-cured, and entirely
  unashamed. The party had almost an entire conversation with him before it
  went wrong. In summary:
  - He was a Bellwarden novice. He dove the crossing the night of the
    drowning and has spent forty years making sure the Bell was never truly
    lost.
  - "The Bell is not a weapon, it is a voice. The coast had a voice before it
    had lighthouses." He considers the wrecks regrettable. He did not deny
    causing them.
  - He has "a patron" with the means to raise what he could only guard. He
    would not give a name and looked, for the first time, briefly afraid.
- Kestrel tried to take him and he went over the terrace wall like a man
  fifty years younger. Lost him in the fog toward the mountains. The road he
  took bends toward Bleakspire Keep, for whatever that is worth.

So: the Salt Factor was Brother Alder all along — every dockhand rumor from
Session 1 belongs to a dead monk. Next: the dredge barge was seen off
Saltmere. If the Bell is coming up, it comes up there.
