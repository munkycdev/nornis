# The Vespergale Reach — demo campaign bible

GM-facing overview of the demo world's campaign. This file is design-time
documentation: it explains the arc, the secrets, and which Nornis feature each
piece of content exists to exercise. The player-visible content lives in
`sessions/`; the two GM-only prep notes at the bottom become GM-visibility
sources in the template.

## Premise

Forty years ago the monks of Greyhollow Abbey drowned a relic — **the Vesper
Bell**, voice of a sea-cult that once ruled this coast — in the flooded river
crossing now called the Sunken Ferry. The order that guarded it, the
**Bellwardens**, was cast out and forgotten. Now somebody is paying wreckers to
dredge the crossing, ships are breaking on a dark coast, and the lighthouse at
Lanternwatch has gone out. The campaign is the party pulling on that thread:
from Harrowport's docks, up the River Silverwithe, through the Mistwood to the
abbey's ruins, and finally — in the held-back Session 6 — to Bleakspire Keep,
where the region's own protector is revealed as the last Bellwarden.

## The party

- **Kestrel Vane** — a coast-born scout who reads tides like other people read faces.
- **Bram Ottery** — boatwright turned sellsword; lost his yard to a wreck he now suspects was no accident.
- **Elowen Marsh** — hedge-witch of the river country; dreams things she'd rather not.
- **Corvin Hale** — disgraced Harrowport customs officer, trying to buy his name back.

## Cast

- **Harbormaster Ilsabet Crane** (Harrowport) — quest-giver; pays the party after the third wreck in a month.
- **Old Fennick** (Lanternwatch) — the missing lighthouse keeper. Found alive in Session 6.
- **Castellan Maren Voss** (Bleakspire Keep) — the Reach's protector and ally-at-a-distance. **Secretly the villain**: last heir of the Bellwardens, funding the dredge. Unrevealed at template snapshot.
- **The Salt Factor** — hooded broker buying drowned charts in Harrowport. **Revealed in Session 4 to be Brother Alder**, a Greyhollow monk listed among the dead. This is the deliberate rename/merge case: sessions 1–3 say "the Salt Factor", session 4 onward says "Brother Alder".
- **Prioress Verene** (Greyhollow, deceased) — ordered the Bell drowned; her scriptorium records are the Session 4 payload.
- **Pike Salloway** and **the Groundling Crew** — wrecker gang doing the dredging; Salloway's captured confession in Session 5 points at keep-minted silver.
- **Tam Rush** (Windrow) — miller's son, river guide.
- **Widow Harrach** (Thornhollow) — folk historian of the Mistwood; identifies the bell-mark.

The template snapshot is the world *as of the end of Session 5*: Bell raised
and carried north, Voss still trusted, Fennick still missing. Session 6 is the
text the tutorial has the user paste.

## GM-only secrets (template GM-visibility sources)

1. **The Castellan's design** — Maren Voss is the last Bellwarden. The
   keep-minted silver paying the Groundlings is hers; the Bell is being carried
   to Bleakspire's cistern-crypt to be hung and sounded at the spring tide
   ("the Undertide"). Old Fennick saw her barge take the light off Lanternwatch
   and is held in the keep's cells. *This is the tutorial's reveal-demo
   content: unrevealed at snapshot, confirmed by Session 6.*
2. **What the Bell does** — sounded over open water it calls the drowned to
   walk the seabed ashore. Verene's generation didn't destroy it because a
   Bellwarden's blood is needed to unhang it; Voss is equally necessary to any
   ending, which is why Session 6 ends with terms rather than a corpse.

## Notes on the notes

The session files are written as a GM's after-session recap: prose with some
bullets, consistent proper names (extraction-friendly), light imperfection
(asides, corrections) so the demo honestly represents messy real input. Names
are all original compounds; no published-setting content. In-world dates use a
simple month name ("Rainmonth") so the timeline has something to hang events on
without inventing a full calendar.
