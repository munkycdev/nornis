# GM prep — The Castellan's design

*(GM-visibility source in the template. Unrevealed at snapshot; Session 6
confirms it. This is the secret the tutorial's "reveal" step demonstrates.)*

Castellan Maren Voss of Bleakspire Keep is the last heir of the Bellwardens —
the cast-out order that once kept the Vesper Bell. The brand on her forearm is
the order's mark, worn under a vambrace for forty years.

Everything traces to her:

- The Groundling Crew's pay is struck at the keep's mint. Brother Alder is her
  broker and true believer, but the silver, the dredge barge, and the soldiers
  at Saltmere are all hers.
- She ordered the Lanternwatch light taken, not broken: the lens is stored in
  the keep and Old Fennick is held alive in the cistern cells. She wants the
  coast dark for the dredge, not littered with dead keepers. The wrecks are
  Alder's freelancing, and she does not know how many there have been.
- The Bell is bound for the cistern-crypt beneath the keep — a drowned
  undercroft older than Bleakspire itself, cut by the old coast faith as a
  hanging vault. The abbey's foundry mount (the party has its twin fitting)
  is already bolted to the vault.
- Her plan: hang the Bell and sound it once at the spring tide — the
  Undertide — to wake the old coast faith as a voice, not a weapon. She
  believes this sincerely. She is wrong about what answers.

Play her as a patriot, not a lunatic: the one authority in the Reach who
actually protects it, spending her own treasury to resurrect the thing her
family died guarding. If confronted with proof of what the Bell raises, she
hesitates — that hesitation is the hinge of the endgame.
