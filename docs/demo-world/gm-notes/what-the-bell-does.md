# GM prep — What the Vesper Bell does

*(GM-visibility source in the template. Never fully revealed to players; the
Session 5 beacon and Fennick's Session 6 account are the only glimpses.)*

The Vesper Bell is the voice of the old coast faith — the drowned religion the
Greyhollow monks buried rather than fight. Sounded over open water, it calls
the drowned: everything the Vespergale has taken walks the seabed toward the
sound. The green calling-fire the Groundlings lit on the Cinder Tor is the
lesser form of the same rite; what it raised off Saltmere, the keep's soldiers
burned on the tide-line before dawn.

Rules of the relic, for consistency:

- It cannot be broken. Prioress Verene's generation tried; her chapter-book
  line "it cannot be broken and it must not be heard" is literal.
- Only a Bellwarden's blood can hang it or unhang it from its mount. This is
  why Voss matters to any ending — and why Alder, a Bellwarden novice, is the
  one person besides her who can take it down. Whoever unhangs it chooses
  where it goes.
- Drowning muffles it but does not silence it. Forty years in the crossing
  leaked enough of its voice to give the Sunken Ferry its underwater lights
  and Elowen Marsh her wet-haired dreams. Deep rock, beyond the tide's reach,
  is true silence — the resolution Session 6 offers.
